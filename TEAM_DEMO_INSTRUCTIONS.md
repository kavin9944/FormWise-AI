# FormWise AI – Teammate Instructions

**You do not need to install anything.**  
Just open the link your team host shares with you.

---

## How to Use FormWise AI

### Step 1 – Open the Link

Your host will share a link that looks like:

```
https://xxxx-xx-xx-xxx-xxx.ngrok-free.app
```

Open it in your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).

> **If you see a warning page from ngrok**, click the grey **"Visit Site"** button to proceed. This is normal – ngrok shows this once per session.

---

### Step 2 – Create an Account

1. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Register"**.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a password.
3. Click **Register**.

---

### Step 3 – Log In

If you already have an account:
1. Click **"Login"**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Login**.

---

### Step 4 – Use the Application

Once logged in, you can:

| Feature | How to access |
|---|---|
| **Upload a form** | Click "Upload Form" in the navigation |
| **AI Analysis** | After upload, click "AI Assistant" |
| **Autofill from profile** | Complete your Student Profile first |
| **Form validation** | Available on the AI Assistant page |
| **Document checklist** | Shown after form analysis |
| **Readiness score** | Shown at the bottom of the assistant page |
| **PDF preview** | Click "Preview" after completing analysis |
| **Download PDF** | Click "Download PDF" on the preview page |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to install Python or any software?**  
A: No. Just open the link in your browser.

**Q: Can I use my phone?**  
A: Yes, the site works on mobile browsers, but Chrome on a laptop gives the best experience.

**Q: My login is not working. What do I do?**  
A: Make sure you are using the exact link your host sent. Try a different browser if the problem continues.

**Q: The link stopped working.**  
A: The host's laptop may be turned off or the demo was stopped. Ask your host to restart `START_PUBLIC_DEMO.bat` and share the new link.

**Q: Will my data be saved?**  
A: Yes. Your account and form data are saved in Firebase and will be there next time you log in.

**Q: Are my uploaded documents safe?**  
A: Uploaded files are stored temporarily on the host's laptop during the demo. They are not shared with anyone else.
